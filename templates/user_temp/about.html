Our Journey

Building a Platform
You Can Trust

From a simple idea to a comprehensive care management system — ElderCare has grown to serve hundreds of families across India.

2022 — The Idea

Inspired by the challenges families face managing elderly care remotely, the concept for ElderCare was born.

2023 — Development

Built on Django's MVT architecture with role-based access, health tracking, and emergency alert modules.

2024 — Launch

Platform launched with doctor and caretaker booking, payment management, and SOS alert system.

2025 — Growth

Expanded to 500+ users with AI-health analysis and mobile app version in development.
