FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a doctor appointment?
Log in to your account, navigate to "Doctors" from the sidebar, select an available doctor, choose your preferred date and time, and submit your booking request. Admin will approve it shortly after review.
What happens when I trigger an SOS alert?
Your SOS alert is instantly stored in our system and the admin receives an immediate notification. The admin will contact you or dispatch emergency assistance based on your registered address and emergency contact information.
How do I make a payment after booking approval?
Once your booking is approved by the admin, the payment option becomes active in your "Bookings" section. You can pay via UPI or Card. The transaction is recorded and viewable in your payment history.
Can I add multiple elder profiles?
Yes! You can add and manage multiple elder profiles under a single account. Each profile maintains separate health records, medicine schedules, and booking history.
Is my data secure on ElderCare?
Absolutely. ElderCare uses Django's built-in authentication, session management, and role-based access control to ensure your personal and medical data is fully secure, private, and never shared without consent.
